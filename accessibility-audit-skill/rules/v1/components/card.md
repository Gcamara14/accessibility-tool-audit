# Card

## MUST
- Card titles that structure content use semantic heading or labeled region patterns.
- Interactive cards expose role/name and keyboard behavior.
- Internal links/actions remain individually discoverable.

## MUST NOT
- Do not use click-anywhere cards without keyboard parity.
- Do not use visual-only card titles as structural headings.

## SHOULD (V1.1 candidates)
- Normalize accessible summary patterns for metric cards.

## Citations
- WCAG 2.1 SC 1.3.1, 2.4.6, 4.1.2
